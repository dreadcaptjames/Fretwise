What's a power chord? The root note and its perfect fifth, sometimes with the root an octave higher. With no third, a power chord is neither major nor minor. Write it as E5, A5, G5, and so on.

01 / THE TECHNIQUE

Build the shape. Move the shape.

01
Find the root

Put your index finger on a note on the low E or A string. That note names your chord: for example, low E string fret 3 = G5.

02
Add the fifth

Place your ring finger two frets higher on the next thicker-to-thinner string. Strike just those two strings.

03
Try the octave

For a thicker three-note shape, put your pinky on the same fret as your ring finger, one string farther toward the floor.

04
Make it punk

Mute unused strings with your fretting hand. Use tight downstrokes, and rest the picking-hand palm lightly by the bridge for punchy palm mutes.
